WARRIORS OF AETHER - FIVE HERO IDEAS
Written 2026-10-17 for the owner. Nothing here is in the game. Every number is my pick; veto or tune any of it.

======================================================================================================================
PART 1. HOW YOUR HEROES ARE BUILT (what I read before designing)
======================================================================================================================

Read: docs/HANDOFF.md, docs/MECHANICS.md, src/sim/sim_data.gd (stats, abilities, talents), sim_bot_kits.gd (roles),
sim_const.gd (elements), sim_status.gd (status effects), the run contracts in work/lemondev/, and the naming rule.
Your own kits: Blood Ranger, Ezee, Azrin, Froster, Caziel, Roydo, Blau, Azrael (plus your rework of Kragg).
Sludge is Dota's Pudge copied 1:1; Tock and Kragg were designed by an agent, so I leaned on them less.

The patterns I found:

1. Four skills, Q W E R. Q is the main damage spell (often a skillshot or area: Burst Shot, Fiery Javelin, Breath of
   Cold, Bounce Shot). W is control or a second tool. E is usually a passive (Masterful Archery, Frost Heart, Dark
   Matter Skin, Death Scythe; Caziel's passive is on W). R has 3 levels at 6/12/18. Most of your ultimates are a timed
   self-buff or a change of form (Blood Ranger, Unholy Might, Anomaly, Divine Darkness); Endless Winter is a channel,
   Divine Smite a nuke, Death Order a passive execute.

2. Each hero has one big idea, and the four skills feed it:
   * Azrin: everything burns, Spirit of Fire charges from her other spells, Divine Smite makes every Fire burn last
     longer, Touch of Ember punishes the enemy for casting.
   * Froster: Ice Pulse lowers defense, everything builds Frost Hearts, Endless Winter freezes.
   * Caziel: dagger in (armor cut), poison procs, shadows to get close, execute at low health.
   * Roydo: Void Rush lowers status resistance so Grip of the Ruler's stun lasts longer.
   * Blau: boulders block paths AND give Bounce Shot more walls to bounce off; the turret copies his attack.
   * Azrael: Soul Tap counts every unit (allies too), the door carries allies, Death Scythe procs off her spells,
     the door blast strips elemental resistance (and she is Dark).

3. You love physics and the map: Gale Impact hurls enemies sideways with crash damage, Dark Stomp's push makes units
   crash into each other, trees and cliffs; Grip throws a unit on an arc; Fiery Javelin drags; Bounce Shot ricochets;
   boulders roll and bounce. Trees fall to spells. Units crashing into things is a shared rule in this game.

4. Chance procs with a cooldown, on Dota's pseudo-random rolls: Masterful Archery (30%, 0.8 s), Death Scythe (1 s),
   Dark Matter Skin, Sweet Poison, Unholy Might.

5. Allies get pulled into enemy spells: Grip can throw an ally (unhurt), Soul Tap counts allies, the door takes two
   allied heroes, Bounce Shot bounces off allies.

6. Some randomness you can still control: Burst Shot's random waves, Grip's random target, the boulders' random angles.

7. Everything has an element (None, Fire, Water, Ice, Earth, Lightning, Nature, Dark, Light), and spells carry it.

8. Counters and stacks you can see: Frost Hearts, spirits, Kragg's Charges, Soul Tap's souls, Overload's attack count.

9. You give the numbers that make the idea work and leave mana, cast ranges, speeds and widths to be "chosen".
   Numbers grow in clean steps (100/140/180/220, 16/15/14/13 s).

10. You want every hero played well by bots, with its own look, effects and a talent tree (4 tiers of 2). The
    Upgrade Staff (Dota's Aghanim's Scepter) can grant a new skill (Pilot Turret).

11. Roles in the bot table now: almost everyone is a core (1/2/3). Only Tock is a real support. Elements in use:
    Fire 1, Ice 1, Dark 3, Lightning 1, Nature 1, Earth 1 (Sludge), Light 1 (Tock), Neutral 2, Water 0.

What I aimed for with these five: fill the gaps (a real save-support, a summoner, a slow heavy hitter), give each one
big idea in the same style as yours, and make each one combo with heroes you already have.

1) AIRAH, THE WIND GODDESS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ranged intelligence support (roles 4, 5, 2). Range 600, projectile 1200.
Str 18 +2.0, Agi 18 +1.8, Int 24 +3.4, damage 21-29, armor 0, speed 300 (fastest support), BAT 1.7, attack point 0.4.
Element: SEE QUESTION A. There is no Wind element. My pick is Lightning (storm sky); the other choice is Neutral.

Big idea: she moves everyone. Enemies get blown into walls, allies get carried out of danger, projectiles get
stopped by the wind.

Q  Gust (point, line)
   A blast of wind 900 long, 200 wide, 1600 speed. Enemies hit take 80/140/200/260 magic damage and are pushed
   300/350/400/450 along the line. An enemy pushed into a tree, cliff, building or unit takes 40/60/80/100 more and is
   stunned 0.6/0.8/1.0/1.2 s (the same crash rule as Dark Stomp and Gale Impact); trees it hits fall.
   Allies on the line are pushed forward 300 too, unhurt, and move 20% faster for 2 s (an escape for a friend).
   12/11/10/9 s, mana 100/110/120/130.

W  Tailwind (unit: ally or self, 800)
   For 5 s the target moves 16/22/28/34% faster, walks through units, and ranged attacks against it miss
   25/30/35/40% of the time (blown aside). Removes slows when cast (basic dispel of slows only).
   16/14/12/10 s, mana 70.

E  Wind Wall (point, 700)
   A wall of wind 600 wide, across her aim, for 4/4.5/5/5.5 s. Enemy projectiles that touch it stop: ranged attacks,
   Shuriken Hook, Fiery Javelin, Gale Impact, Burst Shot arrows, Time Bolt, Bounce Shot. An ALLIED Blau's Bounce Shot
   ricochets off it like a cliff. Enemies walking through it are slowed 30%.
   22/20/18/16 s, mana 90/100/110/120.

R  Hurricane (point 900, radius 450, 6 s; not a channel, she keeps moving and casting)
   Trees inside fall when it forms. Enemies inside are dragged toward the middle at 120/160/200 per second and take
   60/90/120 magic damage per second. When it ends, every enemy still inside is thrown 450 straight out from the
   middle; anyone who crashes into a tree, cliff, building or unit is stunned 1 s. Allies inside move 25% faster.
   90/80/70 s, mana 150/200/250.

How the skills work together: Hurricane gathers them and then throws them. A thrown enemy near a cliff or tree line
crashes and gets stunned, so you place it next to walls. Gust finishes the job (push a flung enemy into a tree for a
second stun). Tailwind and Wind Wall keep your carry alive.
With your heroes: Wind Wall hard counters Sludge's hook and Azrin's javelin; Blau's bullets bounce off it; Hurricane
gathers enemies for Ezee's Dark Stomp (more crashes), Froster's Endless Winter and Kragg's Magnet Field.

Talents: +12% Tailwind speed / +60 Gust damage; -3 s Wind Wall cooldown / +1 s Gust stun; +1.5 s Hurricane /
+20% Tailwind miss chance; Gust hits allies too: +40% speed for them / Hurricane throws 650.
Upgrade Staff: Tailwind hits every allied hero within 600 of the target.
Bot: tries Gust angles that push the target into trees or cliffs (like Blau's bot traces Bounce Shot angles); Tailwind
on an ally who is being chased (the Fade Cape save logic); Wind Wall between an ally and an enemy Sludge, Azrin or
Blood Ranger; Hurricane on 3+ heroes, best when walls are nearby.
Look: your female body floating like Azrin, long pale hair and silk ribbons always blowing, wind spirals at her feet,
leaves and dust caught in her gusts.
Closest to other games (so you can steer away): Wind Wall is close to Yasuo's Wind Wall (League of Legends); the
Hurricane pull is close to Enigma's Black Hole; the throw-out at the end is my own twist.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2) BURAK, THE BERSERKER
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Melee strength carry (roles 1, 3). Two axes.
Str 25 +3.2, Agi 18 +2.1, Int 15 +1.4, damage 30-36, armor 2, speed 305, BAT 1.7, attack point 0.35.
Element: Fire (a temper that burns). Earth would also fit.

Big idea: getting hurt makes him stronger, and he can shrug off disables if he is angry enough. Rage is a stack you
can see, like Frost Hearts or Charges.

Innate  Rage (no point, small slot, like Kragg's Magnetize)
   0-100 Rage. +1 per 1% of his max health lost, +5 per attack he lands on a hero. Loses 10 a second after 4 s
   without hitting or being hit by a hero. Every 10 Rage: +5 attack speed (+50 at full). Shown as a red bar under
   his health and in the buff bar.

Q  Axe Throw (point, line)
   Throws one axe 1000 at 1500 speed, 100 wide. The first enemy hit takes 80/140/200/260 physical damage and is
   slowed 30% for 2 s; the axe sticks in it, or in the first tree or cliff, or drops at the end of its path.
   For 4 s he can cast it again (free) to leap to the axe (0.35 s); he lands next to the target and his next attack
   starts at once. Walking over the dropped axe picks it up and halves the remaining cooldown. While the axe is gone
   he fights with one axe: -20% attack damage. +15 Rage on a hero hit.
   14/13/12/11 s, mana 70/75/80/85.

W  Reckless Swing (no target)
   8 s: his attacks cleave 50/60/70/80% in Dota's cleave cone and lifesteal 15/20/25/30%, but he takes 15% more
   damage (so he builds Rage faster). 16/15/14/13 s, mana 60.

E  Unstoppable (passive)
   When a stun, root or hex lands on him while he has 40+ Rage, he spends 40 Rage and it lasts 40/50/60/70% less
   (status resistance against that one effect, so the game's existing rules handle it).
   12/11/10/9 s cooldown, shown on the E icon. Break stops it.

R  Berserk (no target, 12 s)
   Rage locked at 100. +40/60/80 attack damage, +10% speed, slows do nothing. Half of all damage he takes is held
   back (shown as a dark stretch on his health bar) and comes back as pure damage over 6 s after Berserk ends.
   Every enemy hero he kills or helps kill during Berserk clears the held damage. 70/60/50 s, mana 100/125/150.
   QUESTION B: can the held damage kill him? I wrote yes (he pays for it); the safer choice stops it at 1 health.

How the skills work together: Axe Throw to stick and leap in, Reckless Swing for cleave and lifesteal while the
extra damage he takes fills Rage, full Rage lets Unstoppable shrug off the stun meant to stop him, and Berserk lets him
fight now and pay later unless he gets the kill.
With your heroes: Airah's Tailwind runs him in; Azrael's door drops him on top of a fight; Ezee's Leap Strike and his
leap both pick the hurt target. Counters: Froster's Endless Winter (a frost, many short stuns) and Roydo's Void Rush
(lower status resistance) are the honest answers to Unstoppable.

Talents: +15 attack damage / +150 Axe Throw damage; Rage loses 50% slower / +10% Reckless Swing lifesteal;
-20 Rage needed for Unstoppable / +6 s Axe recast window; +4 s Berserk / held damage comes back over 10 s.
Upgrade Staff: Axe Throw throws both axes in a 20-degree fork; the leap goes to either.
Bot: lanes as a carry; throws an axe at a hero it can follow up on, leaps only when the fight is in its favour
(the team brain's fight model), Reckless Swing on creep waves and in fights, Berserk when the fight starts, not
when it is losing.
Look: a big bare-chested fighter in fur and leather, war paint, two bearded axes, eyes that glow red as Rage fills,
steam off his skin at full Rage.
Closest to other games: the axe throw and pickup is close to Olaf's Undertow (League of Legends); Rage making him
attack faster is near Troll Warlord's Fervor (stacks on one target) and Huskar's Berserker's Blood (scales with
missing health; I am not certain which bonuses it gives in the current patch). Holding damage back for later is my own twist, as far as I know.

3) SER EMELRICH, THE HEAVY SWORD
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Melee strength off-laner and initiator (roles 3, 1, 4). A knight with a huge two-handed sword.
Str 26 +3.4, Agi 13 +1.5, Int 16 +1.6, damage 37-43, armor 3, speed 290, BAT 1.8, attack point 0.55,
turn rate 0.5 (slow and heavy on purpose).
Element: Light (sword of the Ancient Light; Tock is the only other Light hero).

Big idea: slow, readable, crushing. Every big hit has a wind-up the enemy can see, so the skill is landing it. His
skills line enemies up for the next swing.

Q  Overhead Cut (point, line in front)
   0.6 s wind-up (a red line on the ground for everyone, like the Ogre's Smash), then a cut 450 long, 160 wide:
   100/160/220/280 + 60% of his attack damage as physical damage, and a 0.8/1.0/1.2/1.4 s stun. Trees on the line fall.
   13/12/11/10 s, mana 90/100/110/120.

W  Sweep (no target, half circle in front, 300 radius)
   A wide swing: his attack damage + 40/70/100/130 physical. Enemies hit are shoved 200 along the swing toward his
   left, so they pile up. Anyone shoved into a tree, cliff, building or unit takes 40/60/80/100 and is stunned 0.5 s.
   11 s, mana 80/85/90/95.

E  Steel Stance (toggle, 1 s between toggles, no mana)
   While on: -20% speed, half turn rate, +4/6/8/10 armor, 25/35/45/55% less damage from attacks and line shots that
   come from his front 120 degrees, and enemy pushes and pulls do not move him.
   Turning matters, so how you face is part of how you play him.

R  Giant Swing (point, aims a cone)
   1.2 s wind-up (a red cone shown to everyone), then a 700-long, 100-degree cut: 250/400/550 physical damage that
   ignores 50% of armor, and every enemy hit is knocked down (stunned) for 1.2/1.5/1.8 s. Every tree in the cone
   falls. The wind-up breaks on stun, hex or silence, but with Steel Stance on only hex and silence break it.
   70/60/50 s, mana 150/200/250.

How the skills work together: Sweep piles them to one side, Overhead Cut pins one, Giant Swing cuts the pile. Steel
Stance is the price and the protection: he can't be pushed out of the wind-up, but he moves and turns like a wall.
With your heroes: Airah's Hurricane and Kragg's Magnet Field hand him a pile; Blau's boulders and traps hold
enemies inside the red cone; Feran's Shuffle (below) scrambles them inside it. Counters: Ezee's Dark Stomp and
Kragg's Repel push him out of position, unless Steel Stance is on.

Talents: +250 health / +1 s Overhead Cut stun; +60 Sweep damage / Sweep shoves 350; Steel Stance blocks from
the sides too (180 degrees) / -0.3 s Overhead Cut wind-up; Giant Swing ignores all armor / -20 s Giant Swing.
Upgrade Staff: Giant Swing also swings back the other way 0.5 s later (a second, smaller cone, half damage).
Bot: casts the wind-ups only on a target that is stunned, rooted, slowed or out of escape spells (it knows Leap
Strike, Jump Knife, Door of the End and Lay Trap leaps); Sweep into walls; Steel Stance on when a ranged or line-shot
hero is in front of him and he is not chasing.
Look: full plate on the hero body, white and gold, a tabard with the Ancient Light sun, an oversized two-handed sword
with a long leather grip; sparks and a short ground crack on every heavy hit.
Note: "Heavy Sword" was once the name of the Sange item (it is Kugmo now), so there is no clash in the shop, but a few
code comments still say "the Heavy Sword family" (sim_unit.gd, sim_world.gd, MECHANICS.md). Worth renaming those to
"the Kugmo family" if he goes in.
Closest to other games: the front block is near Mars's Bulwark (Dota); the telegraphed cuts are like the Ogre's Smash
already in this game; I know of no Dota hero with Sweep's sideways pile-up.

4) FERAN MOCK, THE JESTER
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ranged intelligence trickster (roles 4, 2, 5). Range 550, projectile 1100 (thrown knives).
Str 18 +1.9, Agi 20 +2.2, Int 23 +3.0, damage 20-26, armor 1, speed 305, BAT 1.7, attack point 0.35.
Element: Neutral (a jester belongs to nobody).

Big idea: chaos you can still steer. Random knives, random pranks, swapped places, a shuffled teamfight. Like
Burst Shot and Grip of the Ruler, the randomness has rules, so a good player still wins with it.

Q  Knife Juggle (point, 700)
   Throws 4/5/6/7 knives, 0.1 s apart, each at a random angle within 25 degrees of the aim, 800 reach, 1200 speed,
   60 wide. Each knife deals 45/60/75/90 magic damage; one enemy is hit by at most 3 knives per cast.
   9 s, mana 80/90/100/110.

W  Joke Box (point, 600)
   Throws a box that lands and pops after 0.8 s (a visible fuse) in a 275 area: 60/100/140/180 magic damage, and each
   enemy rolls its own prank:
     * Pie: its attacks miss 50% of the time for 3 s.
     * Spring: thrown 350 in a random direction (crash rule applies: trees, cliffs and units stun 0.6 s).
     * Confetti: silenced 1.5/2/2.5/3 s.
     * Glue: rooted 1/1.3/1.6/1.9 s.
   14/13/12/11 s, mana 100/110/120/130.

E  Swap (unit, any hero, ally or enemy, 700/800/900/1000)
   He and the target trade places at once (projectiles aimed at either miss). A swapped enemy is turned to face away
   and slowed 40% for 1.5 s; a swapped ally gets a basic dispel. 20/18/16/14 s, mana 70.

R  Shuffle (point 700, area 450, 0.5 s cast)
   Every enemy hero and creep inside trades places with another random enemy inside (heroes can land in the middle
   of their creeps or on the front line), each is stunned 0.8/1.0/1.2 s, then dizzy for 3 s (half turn rate, 25%
   slower). If only one enemy is inside, it trades places with Feran instead. 80/70/60 s, mana 150/200/250.

How the skills work together: Swap pulls an enemy carry into your team; Shuffle breaks the enemy formation (the
carry in front, the support at the back); Joke Box and Knife Juggle punish the confused pile. Dizzy matters because
turn rate is real in this game (the turn rates in sim_data.gd).
With your heroes: Shuffle and Swap set up Emelrich's Giant Swing and Froster's Endless Winter; Swap saves an ally
from Sludge's Chomp or Caziel's dive; Spring prank near Blau's boulders or a tree line stuns.

Talents: +1 knife / +8% magic amp; Joke Box rolls two pranks on each enemy / -3 s Swap; +200 Swap range /
+60 Joke Box damage; +1 s Shuffle stun / Shuffle radius +150.
Upgrade Staff: Swap can target an allied hero and an enemy hero together: those two trade places (Feran stays put).
Bot: Knife Juggle at point blank (more knives hit); Joke Box on a group or a fleeing hero; Swap the enemy hero its
team is chasing into its allies, or swap out an ally under a stun; Shuffle on 3+ enemies when its team is ready.
Look: a lean jester in a red and black patched coat, a three-point bell hat, a painted half-smile mask, knives on
a belt; confetti, bells and a puff of coloured smoke on Swap.
New systems he needs: a "miss chance" debuff (Pie; Dota calls this blind) and the place swap.
Closest to other games: Swap is close to Vengeful Spirit's Nether Swap (Dota); I know of no hero with Shuffle or
the random pranks.

5) GHORMAN, THE BEAST KING
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Melee strength off-laner / jungler (roles 3, 4). A huge wild man in furs who rules the animals.
Str 24 +3.0, Agi 17 +1.9, Int 18 +1.8, damage 30-36, armor 1, speed 305, BAT 1.7, attack point 0.4.
Element: Nature (beasts). Nature beats Water and Earth.

Big idea: he leads a pack. He takes over the game's own neutral creeps, so the camps you built (Ogre Smash, Orc
Shaman Stomp, Alpha Wolf aura, Goblin Digger disarm) become his tools, and he gets stronger the more beasts he has.

Q  Tame (unit, 600: a neutral creep (not from the ancient camp) or an enemy lane creep)
   It becomes his for good, with +20/30/40/50% health and attack damage, and keeps its own ability. He can own 1/2/2/3
   at once; taming past the cap releases the oldest (it dies). 30/26/22/18 s, mana 70.

W  Roar (no target, 500)
   Enemies are slowed 35/40/45/50% and deal 15/20/25/30% less attack damage for 3 s. His beasts within 900 get +30
   attack speed and 20% speed for 6 s. 15 s, mana 90/100/110/120.

E  Pack Leader (passive)
   Each of his beasts within 900 gives him +3/4/5/6 attack damage and +1/1.5/2/2.5 armor. When one of his beasts
   dies, he heals 8/10/12/14% of his max health.

R  Beast Form (no target, 25 s; his model swaps, as Roydo's Anomaly does)
   +250/400/550 health, +40/60/80 attack damage, +40 speed; every 3rd attack is a maul for 100/150/200 extra physical
   damage and a 0.6 s bash (the bash rule: only one bash source counts). His beasts grow too: +25% health and damage,
   and their ability cooldowns run 50% faster (the game's cooldown rate system). 80/70/60 s, mana 100/125/150.

How the skills work together: Tame builds the pack, Pack Leader turns the pack into his armor and damage, Roar makes
the pack and him hit first, Beast Form makes all of them bigger and makes the Ogre Smash or Orc Stomp come back twice
as fast.
With your heroes: an Alpha Wolf's aura helps carries like Blood Ranger and Blau; Kragg's Charges and Ezee's Unholy
Might work well with lots of units hitting at once; Azrael's Soul Tap counts his beasts (more souls).
Counters: area spells (Burst Shot, Endless Winter, Stench) kill the pack.

Talents: +1 beast / +20 Roar attack speed; +15% tamed health and damage / -6 s Tame; +2 Pack Leader armor per
beast / Roar radius +200; +10 s Beast Form / maul every 2nd attack.
Upgrade Staff: Tame works on ancient camp creeps (the Lava Golem and Fire Imps), still 1 at a time for those.
Bot: tames a large camp creep with a useful ability first (Ogre for Smash, Orc Shaman for Stomp), lanes with it,
farms jungle camps with the pack, Roar when it engages, Beast Form when the team fights. Needs bot control of extra
units (Blau's turret aims on its own; tamed creeps would have to follow orders).
Look: a giant bearded man with a lion-mane cloak, bone and antler armour, a big club; in Beast Form a huge maned beast,
part bear, part lion; green spirit light on his tamed creeps.
New systems he needs: owning and ordering extra units (select, move, attack; I have not checked whether the HUD can
select units besides the hero and courier yet).
Closest to other games: taking over creeps is close to Chen's Holy Persuasion and Enchantress's Enchant (Dota) and
the Dominator item; Beast Form growing his units too is close to Lycan's Shapeshift (I am not certain of every detail
of Lycan's current version, check before using it as a reference).

======================================================================================================================
PART 3. QUESTIONS FOR YOU, AND WHAT IS NOT CHECKED
======================================================================================================================

QUESTION A (Airah's element): the game has no Wind element. Options: (1) Lightning (my pick: storm and sky),
(2) Neutral, like Roydo and Blau, (3) add a Wind element. Option 3 means changing the whole element table in
sim_const.gd (who Wind beats and who beats Wind), so every other hero's matchups shift.

QUESTION B (Burak's Berserk): can the damage that comes back after Berserk kill him?

QUESTION C (roles): the roster is mostly cores. I made Airah and Feran supports and Emelrich and Ghorman
off-laners on purpose. Tell me if you meant any of them as carries or mids.

QUESTION D (genders): I wrote Airah as female (Goddess) and Burak, Emelrich ("Ser"), Feran and Ghorman ("King") as
male because of the titles. Correct me if any is meant differently.

Naming: "Tailwind" is an ordinary English word, but tools/check_names.py flags it (it reads "wind" glued onto
"tail" as a made-up compound). If you like the name, add "tailwind" to COMPOUND_OK in the checker; if not,
"Wind Boost" passes. Every other ability name above passes the checker. If any of these go in, they also have to be
listed in docs/HANDOFF.md for your veto, as the rule says.

New systems these heroes would need, none of which exist yet as far as I can tell from the code: a wall that stops
projectiles (Wind Wall), damage held back and taken later (Berserk), damage blocked from the front (Steel Stance),
a miss chance debuff (Pie), place swapping (Swap, Shuffle), and owning and ordering tamed units (Tame).
Already in the game and reused: crash damage and stuns from pushes, falling trees, Dota's cleave cone, pseudo-random
procs, status resistance, bash rules, the cooldown rate system, model swaps (Anomaly), toggles, innates, the
Upgrade Staff granting changes.

Not checked: none of this is built or play-tested. The numbers are my guesses next to your heroes' numbers and the
stat templates, not balance-tested; bot duels would show whether any of them is too strong. The "closest to other
games" notes are from memory of Dota 2 and League of Legends, which change with patches; check them against current
patch notes before you use them to steer away from a copy.
