==Additional Information==
* A full set of Tangos is not [[Item sharing|shareable]]. However, it is possible to give allies a charge, {{I|Tango (Shared)}}, by casting it on them.

==Trivia==
* The {{emote|Herald}} [[Matchmaking/Seasonal Rankings|Herald Rank]]'s symbol is based on Tango.
* In the original DotA, Tango's full name was "Ancient Tango of Essifation".
* Tango's active ability was called "Eat a Tree or Ward" before [[July 19, 2017 Patch]].
* {{I|Ancient Knowledge Stone}} may have inspired the creation of Tango.
