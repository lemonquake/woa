==Trivia==
* This item is based on the [https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Ironwood_Branch Ironwood Branch] from {{wc3}}.
** In DotA, the item is named <i>Ironwood Branch</i> as well. [https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Ironwood Ironwood] is a sturdy type of wood from the Warcraft universe.
** The name was shortened to just <i>Iron Branch</i>, possibly to avoid copyright issues with "Blizzard Entertainment".
* The ''happy little trees'' in the active ability description is a reference to [[wikipedia:Bob Ross|Bob Ross]], a popular painter who often used the phrase on his television program, [[wikipedia:The Joy of Painting|''The Joy of Painting'']].
* The planted tree uses the same model as the {{A|Nature's Prophet|Sprout}} trees and is surrounded by the same effects as a tree enchanted with {{A|Treant Protector|Eyes In The Forest}}.
* Typing in <code>GG</code> into the shop search bar will bring up the Iron Branch, in reference to their fan name of ''GG Branches'' due to DotA 1 giving them the description of <code>Buying one of these will ensure a good game.</code>.
