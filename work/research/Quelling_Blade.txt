** The attack damage bonus values are considered by [[critical strike]], [[cleave]], [[splash]], [[lifesteal]] and with certain abilities (e.g. {{A|Templar Assassin|Psi Blades}} and {{A|Luna|Moon Glaives}})

** Since it is considered bonus attack damage, it is not affected by percentage-based attack damage bonuses or reductions.

* The attack damage bonus adapts immediately depending on the wielder's range type ({{Symbol|Melee}} [[melee]] or {{Symbol|Ranged}} [[ranged]]).
