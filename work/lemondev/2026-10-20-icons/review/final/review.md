# Blind visual review

Scope: only the six supplied PNG galleries in final/A and final/B. Both sets show 93 skills and 134 item states, with paired large and small icons and representative dim/cooldown treatments. This is a visual comparison; icons cannot prove damage types, thresholds, passive disabling, or other mechanics.

## Skills

- Choice: A
- clear_advantage: true
- Acceptance: rejected against the complete frozen criterion.

A has a clear advantage in dimensional materials, directional light, and cohesive painted rendering. Steel Stance reads as a lit metal shield, Place Turret as a machine, Stop Time as a clock, and Wind Wall as a vertical barrier. B often has cleaner simple silhouettes, but its flat symbols and repeated radial backgrounds do not meet the painted-material criterion as well. A's large effects sometimes converge into colored texture at 32 px; the blue cold skills and several purple attacks require their labels more than the clearest object icons do.

All 93 occupied skill cells and their small partners are present and render without visible corruption in both sets. Cooldown and dim treatments are visible.

The mandatory semantic check is not fully satisfied. In A, Death Order shows a blade and red impact, but a weak target is not legible in the small icon. Beast Form reads as a snarling beast, but the transformation into a werewolf is ambiguous. Break Strike reads as an armored impact with a yellow effect; disabling a passive is not distinguishable. Weapon Grab shows weapon contact and green surrounding forms, but releasing or removing an opponent's weapon is not clear at 32 px. B's target/sword, animal emblem, broken shield, and small falling weapons do not collectively resolve these four requirements either. Their labels help identify intent, but that is weaker than the requested visual clarity.

Largest remaining gap: the four required skills do not communicate sufficiently distinct target/action cues at the small size.

Concrete recheck: revise these four icons around a single large action silhouette, then show the same raw 74 px and 32 px pairs with the existing dim/cooldown treatments. Check whether a reviewer can distinguish a strike on an injured target, a human-to-wolf change, a passive being interrupted, and a weapon leaving a hand without consulting labels. No icon-only check should be treated as proof of the underlying mechanics.

## Items

- Choice: A
- clear_advantage: true
- Acceptance: accepted for the supplied visual criterion, with the remaining readability limitation below.

A makes the objects more recognizable as physical things through metal reflections, leather/fur texture, glass, and shaped lighting. Chain Shirt, Heavy Belt, Fur Hat, Halberd, Teleport Scroll, and Travel Boots provide concrete examples. The set is visually cohesive. B has useful broad silhouettes, but some substitutions are much less literal: Headband resembles a crown, Healing Paste a potion, and Steel Hammer a cluster of crystals.

All 134 occupied item-state cells and their small partners are present and render without visible corruption in both sets. Unoccupied space on the second page is ordinary unused gallery space.

Mandatory states pass in A. Blast Rod 1 through 5 have ascending, countable rows of one through five pips; the pip rows remain visible on the supplied small versions, and the darkened large versions retain their count. The red, green, and blue Switch Boots states are distinct in both sizes, with shape/detail variation supplementing the color. Full Flask contains prominent blue liquid, while Empty Flask has a clear center and glass highlights; the distinction remains visible in the small partners. B also supplies distinct boot colors, ascending pips, and empty/full flask states.

Largest remaining gap: several blue mana objects and purple weapons collapse toward similar bright masses at 32 px, especially with dim overlays. Mana Gem, Mana Stone, and Mana Tiara are more distinct at the large size than at the small size.

Follow-up check: retain the painted finish while testing stronger silhouettes and quieter surrounding effects for those similar objects, using the same gallery size and overlays.
