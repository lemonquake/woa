## Plan and how to do each part

### D1 Debug panel (owner item 1)
* Cause: the panel is placed at `screen width - W` but the framed theme box adds margins, so the real panel is wider
  than `W` and runs off the right edge; its height ignores the box margins and the bottom panel, so the last rows and
  the frame's lower ornaments are cut.
* Fix: size the panel from its real size every frame (`panel.size`), keep it inside the screen with a margin, cap the
  scroll height to the room above the bottom panel, let buttons shrink to the column width (no 150 px minimum that
  forces the width), and keep every section reachable by scrolling.
* Check: open it (F9) at 1280x720, 1920x1080 and 3840x2160 (`--4k` probes or a real 4K window); every button visible
  or reachable by scroll, nothing outside the screen. Capture to `work/lemondev/2026-10-15b-ui-items-heroes/shots/`.

### S1 Auto-select the hero on a command (owner item 2)
* In `input_controller.gd`: when no unit the player controls is selected (an enemy, a creep, a building or nothing),
  a right click on the world or minimap, Stop, Hold, Attack, Teleport, an ability key or an item key first selects the
  hero (`select([hero])`) and then runs the command. With the courier selected, the courier keeps its own commands.
* Check: a test or probe that selects an enemy creep, right clicks the ground, and sees the hero move and selected.

### S2 Any selected unit in the bottom panel (owner item 3)
* `hud.shown()` returns the selected unit of any kind. `BottomPanel` draws a unit that is not a hero with the same
  console: name plate and level, portrait (3D camera framed on the unit's model; buildings framed on their bounds),
  health and mana bars with regen, damage, armor, move speed, attack range, attack speed, magic resistance, bounty and
  XP, its element / damage / armor chips and the full stat sheet, its abilities and auras and on-hit effects shown as
  skill slots (neutral spells Snarl, Stomp, Smash; auras; Crit; Splash; Weapon Grab; Break Strike; tower true sight
  and backdoor), and its status icons. Items area hidden for units without an inventory. The old small card goes.
* Check: captures of a lane creep, a neutral with abilities, a tower, the Throne and an enemy hero.

### S3 Dynamic skill bar, up to 10 skills (owner item 4)
* The ability row is laid out from the hero's ability count (innate abilities shown as a small slot, not counted).
  Up to 4 slots: today's size. 5-6: same size, the console widens. 7-10: slots shrink (minimum 52 px) so the
  console stays within 1360 px. Items, gold and Shop move right with it. The console art is drawn in three parts
  (left, a stretched middle under the abilities, right) so the frame is never squashed.
* Hotkeys: 10 ability actions. Up to 4 skills: Q W E R. More: Dota's layout, the last skill (the ultimate) stays on
  R and the extra ones take D F G Y U I in order (6 skills: Q W E D F R). All rebindable on the Hotkeys screen.
* Check: a debug or capture flag that gives a hero 6 and 10 skills; captures at 1080p and 4K; clicks hit each slot;
  the learn "+" buttons, level pips and tooltips line up.

### I1 Cooldown system (owner item 5)
* Cooldown reduction (Octarine Core 25%): every ability and item cooldown is multiplied by (1 - reduction) when it
  starts; several sources do not stack (largest counts, as Dota's Octarine). Stat key `cooldown_reduction_max`.
* Cooldown rate (new): a modifier stat `cooldown_rate` changes how fast cooldowns run down: +0.5 = 50% faster,
  -0.4 = 40% slower, -1 = frozen. Applied each tick by shifting `ability_ready` and item `ready` times. This is the
  "amplify" side (enemy debuffs make cooldowns run slower) and the new heroes use it.
* AoE bonus (Chasm Stone family: Gleipnir, Shiva's) and cast range bonus (Aether Lens, Dagon) applied in
  `SimAbilities.val()` to radius and range keys.
* Check: headless tests for each: an Octarine hero's cooldown is 75%; a slowed cooldown runs 40% slower; a frozen
  one does not move; two Octarines do not stack.

### H1, H2 Two new heroes (owner item 7). Names are ours (owner may veto)

**Tock, the Clockmaker**: ranged intelligence, Light. Six skills (shows the bigger skill bar): an old tinkerer who
bends time. Synergy: his spells slow enemy cooldowns and speed up his allies'; every spell he casts winds his others
forward; Stop Time freezes a fight (and the enemies' cooldowns) for his team to clean up, Rewind takes him out.
* Q **Time Bolt** (line): magic damage; enemies hit have their cooldowns run 30/40/50/60% slower for 4 s.
* W **Fast Forward** (ally or self): for 5 s the ally's cooldowns run 40/60/80/100% faster and it moves faster.
* E **Rewind** (no target): back to where he stood 3 s ago with the health and mana he had then (basic dispel).
* D **Slow Field** (area): 5 s field that slows enemies' movement and attacks.
* F **Quick Hands** (passive): each spell he casts cuts his other cooldowns by 1/1.5/2/2.5 s.
* R **Stop Time** (area): enemies in the area are frozen (stunned, their cooldowns stop) for 2.5/3/3.5 s.

**Kragg, the Magnet Knight**: melee strength, Lightning. Five skills: a knight in iron plate who is a living magnet.
Synergy: everything he does puts Charges on enemies; charged enemies are pulled harder, thrown further and cashed
out by Overload.
* Q **Iron Grip** (enemy): he and the target snap together at the midpoint; damage, short stun, +2 Charges.
* W **Repel** (no target): knocks enemies around him back (further per Charge), slows; crashing into walls stuns.
* E **Static Charge** (passive): attacks add a Charge (up to 5, 10 s); charged enemies take more damage from his
  spells.
* D **Overload** (no target): every charged enemy within 800 takes damage per Charge (Charges consumed); he heals
  for part of it.
* R **Magnet Field** (no target): 6 s field that drags enemies within 700 toward him (harder with Charges); at the
  end enemies within 300 are stunned.
* Each hero: talents (4 tiers), a look on the customisable hero body (outfit, dyes, weapon), Hero Select text,
  ability icons, visual effects for every skill, bot kit (roles, skill order, build, combo, escapes), and tests for
  each ability. Check: input probe for every skill, 12-min bot duels against every hero (no script errors), a 5 v 5
  bot game with both heroes.
