# Icon audit

Owner update after this initial audit: "No! Change the Death Order". The retained-original instruction below is
historical and revoked. Current generation scope is34 images, including a new Death Order. Final coverage is in
`after/audit.json`; all259 expected unique painted sources are present and all305 runtime routes agree.

Read-only audit dated 2026-10-22. Existing names are copied exactly; no new names or gameplay changes.

## Result

- 120 live ability/unit icon entries: 92 painted PNGs, 27 new hero abilities still using SVG fallback, and Death Order intentionally using its original SVG.
- Five additional current-sword buff states use SVGs and are absent from the dump.
- Generate 32 missing skill/state identities and replace the stale Giant Swing image: 33 candidates total.
- 129 items, 47 recipes and four additional held states resolve to 134 unique painted item identities. Missing item artwork: zero.
- All existing item definitions match the prior audit; retain their artwork, including Blast Rod levels, Switch Boots states, Empty Flask, and recipe sharing.
- Retain Ghosts of the Will: its faster travel and instant impact are already expressed by the flying-sword-ghost art.
- KEEP ORIGINAL Death Order. Do not reopen that choice.

## Routes and secondary actions

Other new hero abilities already have identity-preserving `art/skills/<id>` routes and will resolve new PNGs after import.

Pilot Turret/Leave Turret and Door of the End exit placement reuse their existing skill artwork. Holy Beam turn/Stop are orders, not additional skill identities. Slow Field, Quick Hands, Pilot Turret and Blessing of the Beast King extra skills already have artwork. Dracoxinia has Q/W/E/D/F/R plus her innate; the sword buffs add five more icons.

Flask charge counts and stored-orb colors are dynamic overlays on existing art. Empty Flask and Switch Boots attribute states already have unique painted PNGs. Blast Rod levels already have distinct PNGs.
